About us

We started in Rumeli Kavağı, in a wooden boat.

Our story began in a wooden boat at Rumeli Kavağı, Istanbul, where the Bosphorus opens onto the Black Sea. For four generations since, the sea has passed from father to son.

Today our three purse seiners are tied to no single port; we go wherever the fish are, in the Black Sea, the Aegean and the Mediterranean. Grandfather Dursun's name is painted on our bows: DURSUN ÇINAROĞLU.

What we do

Purse seining: the sea's most selective catch.

A purse seine encircles a school of pelagic fish near the surface and closes beneath it like a drawstring bag. Target species are chosen; the seabed is never touched.

  1. Find the school

    Hours on the bridge at the sonar and echo sounder: the school's position, depth and size are read.

  2. Set the net

    The vessel draws a full circle around the school, paying out hundreds of metres of net within minutes.

  3. Purse and haul

    The purse line is drawn, closing the net like a bag; the crew hauls it aboard with the power block.

  4. Into the crate, fresh

    The catch goes into chilled holds, then into crates, and reaches port in prime condition.

Bluefin tuna

Giant of the Mediterranean.

Atlantic bluefin tuna, Thunnus thynnus

Every year between late May and the end of June, bluefin is fished by purse seine off Malta, in international waters, under ICCAT quota and the Ministry's implementing rules.

The school is moved alive into a transfer cage without leaving the water, and counted and sized by stereo camera. Our own purse seiners then tow the cage to the farm: the same vessels both make the catch and carry it. Every trip is supervised by an independent ICCAT observer.
